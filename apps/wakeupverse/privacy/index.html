Wake Up Verse

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Wake Up Verse handles information in the app.

Effective date: November 16, 2026 (updated for the free trial and in-app purchase)

In brief: The app is designed to keep your saved verses, alarms, playlists, settings, and prepared speech audio on your device. It does not require a VersaGal account. The free trial and the optional one-time purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store.

Information stored on your device

Wake Up Verse stores the information needed to provide its features, including verses you add or save, favorites, alarm times and repeat settings, playlist choices, theme and text-size preferences, and audio prepared for playback. This information is kept in the app’s local storage. It is not uploaded to a VersaGal Digital account or server by the current app.

Optional Bible reference search

If you use the online World English Bible reference search, the requested reference is sent to bible-api.com to retrieve matching text. Do not enter personal or sensitive information in a reference search. The provider states that its server logs may include your IP address, request time, requested URL and query parameters, response status, browser or client information, referring URL, and server errors; it says those logs are deleted after 30 days and that no other data is retained. Review the provider’s current privacy and service information before using the search. You can instead use bundled verses or add text manually.

Daily Verse

If you choose Daily Verse for an alarm, the app looks up each day's verse in the World English Bible through bible-api.com, using a reference from the app's built-in list (for example, “Psalm 23:1”). Only that reference and normal connection information, such as your IP address, are sent, and the provider's logging practice described above applies. The verse text is saved on your device so it can play offline. If you don't use Daily Verse, no daily lookup happens.

Speech and ambient audio

Speech is generated or played by the selected on-device voice or audio source. Wake Up Verse does not send verse text to a VersaGal Digital speech server. Ambient tracks included with the app are played locally. The operating system and voice provider may process information according to their own terms when their services are used.

Free trial and in-app purchase

Wake Up Verse offers a free 7-day trial and an optional one-time purchase that unlocks the full app. Both are handled by Apple through the App Store. Starting the trial is recorded as a free in-app purchase so the App Store can remember when it began, including after you reinstall the app. The app reads this trial and purchase status from the App Store on your device to decide which features are available. We do not receive your payment details, and checking your purchase does not send your verses, alarms, or settings to us. Apple handles purchase information under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Alarms, notifications, and permissions

The app uses operating-system alarm, notification, and audio features to provide alarms and reminders. Your device may display permission prompts and may store alarm schedules in its own system services. Those system services are governed by Apple’s or Google’s privacy terms and settings.

Voices and Nightstand

To let you choose a voice, the app reads the list of voices installed on your device; that list stays on your device. Nightstand mode keeps the screen on while it is showing and does not collect any information.

Diagnostics, analytics, and advertising

The current app does not require an account and does not include advertising or a VersaGal Digital analytics profile. If a future version adds diagnostics, analytics, or connected services, this policy will be updated before that version is distributed.

Retention and deletion

Your locally stored app information remains on the device until you remove it in the app, clear the app’s data using device settings, or uninstall the app. Device backups may preserve app data according to your operating-system backup settings. The third-party Bible lookup provider handles its request records under its own retention policy.

Children’s privacy

Wake Up Verse is a general audience app and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians can contact us with privacy questions.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above will be revised when an update is posted.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@versagaldigital.com. For app support, contact support@versagaldigital.com.
