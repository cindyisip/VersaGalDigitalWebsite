Support

Help with your verse library, alarms, listening, and reading settings.

Contact support
Email support@versagaldigital.com. If you can, include your device model, operating system version, app version, and the steps you took. Do not include private verse text unless it is needed to explain the issue.

Common questions

How do the free trial and the unlock work?

Wake Up Verse is free to download. You can try everything free for 7 days; nothing is charged automatically and there is no subscription. After the trial, the free version keeps one alarm and one verse (you can change that verse once). A one-time purchase of US$4.99 unlocks every verse, Daily Verse alarms, your own verses, unlimited alarms, Random Favorites, Repeat during the day, and the full playlist and Nightstand rotation. Regional prices and taxes may vary; Apple shows the final price before you confirm.

I already bought it. How do I unlock it again?

Open Settings → Full version and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought it with. A new iPhone or a reinstall needs this once. Alarms that the free version switched off turn back on after you unlock.

Why can’t I schedule alarms on my iPhone?

Alarms use Apple’s alarm system, which needs iOS 26 or later. On earlier versions, your alarms are saved and start ringing after you update; the playlist, Nightstand, and your verses work meanwhile. To update, open Settings → General → Software Update. Not every iPhone can install iOS 26; Apple’s update guide explains how to check.

What is Daily Verse?

In Edit alarm, choose Daily Verse instead of a single verse. The alarm then reads a different encouraging World English Bible verse each day. The app prepares a week of verses at a time and adds more whenever you open it, so open Wake Up Verse at least once a week. The verse text is downloaded once and saved on your phone. Daily Verse is part of the full version.

Can an alarm ring several times a day?

Yes. In the alarm’s time card, turn on Repeat during the day and choose how often (every 15 minutes up to every 12 hours) and an end time. The card lists the times it will ring, up to 48 a day.

The voice sounds robotic. How do I get a better one?

Download a free natural voice: open iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Read & Speak (Spoken Content on iOS 17–18) → Voices → English, and download a Premium or Enhanced voice such as Ava, Tom, Zoe, or Evan. Back in Wake Up Verse, pick it under Voice in Edit alarm (each alarm can have its own) or under Settings → Reading voice, and tap Preview voice.

What is Nightstand?

On the Playlist tab, tap ☾ Nightstand for a silent bedside clock that shows a new verse every 1 to 60 minutes. Tap the screen for Next verse, timing, brightness, and Exit. The screen stays on, so keep your phone charging. A scheduled alarm still rings over Nightstand.

How do I add a verse?

Open My Verses and tap ＋ Add. Choose a category, search a supported Bible reference when available, or enter a verse and its source manually. Saved items are the choices available to alarms and playlists.

How do I choose a verse for an alarm?

Edit or create an alarm and choose Choose a Verse to select one from My Verses. Select Random Favorites to have the app choose from favorited verses in enabled categories.

Why can’t I hear the spoken verse?

  • Check the phone’s media volume and current audio output, such as Bluetooth headphones or a speaker.
  • In the device’s accessibility or speech settings, make sure the selected voice is available and its language data is installed.
  • Try the in-app voice preview with ambient audio turned off. If the preview is still silent, email support with the status shown and your device/iOS or Android version.

Why is the alarm quiet or not starting?

Check the alarm is enabled, review the operating system’s notification and alarm permissions for Wake Up Verse, and confirm the selected sound and volume. Battery, focus, and background restrictions can vary by device. If it continues, send support the exact alarm time and device details.

Can I use the app without internet?

Bundled verses and verses already saved on your device are available offline. Online Bible reference search needs a connection. Spoken audio is prepared on the device for later listening where the selected voice and platform allow it.

Can I change the look and text size?

Yes. In Settings, choose Forest or Night Glow and adjust Reading Comfort to make verse text easier to read.

How do I control ambient music?

Ambient audio is optional. Turn it off or choose a track in the listening or alarm controls. Keep the phone’s media volume at a comfortable level.
