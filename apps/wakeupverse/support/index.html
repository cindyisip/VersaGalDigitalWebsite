Support

Help with your verse library, alarms, listening, and reading settings.

Contact support
Email support@versagaldigital.com. If you can, include your device model, operating system version, app version, and the steps you took. Do not include private verse text unless it is needed to explain the issue.

Common questions

How do I add a verse?

Open My Verses and tap ＋ Add. Choose a category, search a supported Bible reference when available, or enter a verse and its source manually. Saved items are the choices available to alarms and playlists.

How do I choose a verse for an alarm?

Edit or create an alarm and choose Choose a Verse to select one from My Verses. Select Random Favorites to have the app choose from favorited verses in enabled categories.

Why can’t I hear the spoken verse?

  • Check the phone’s media volume and current audio output, such as Bluetooth headphones or a speaker.
  • In the device’s accessibility or speech settings, make sure the selected voice is available and its language data is installed.
  • Try the in-app voice preview with ambient audio turned off. If the preview is still silent, email support with the status shown and your device/iOS or Android version.

Why is the alarm quiet or not starting?

Check the alarm is enabled, review the operating system’s notification and alarm permissions for Wake Up Verse, and confirm the selected sound and volume. Battery, focus, and background restrictions can vary by device. If it continues, send support the exact alarm time and device details.

Can I use the app without internet?

Bundled verses and verses already saved on your device are available offline. Online Bible reference search needs a connection. Spoken audio is prepared on the device for later listening where the selected voice and platform allow it.

Can I change the look and text size?

Yes. In Settings, choose Forest or Night Glow and adjust Reading Comfort to make verse text easier to read.

How do I control ambient music?

Ambient audio is optional. Turn it off or choose a track in the listening or alarm controls. Keep the phone’s media volume at a comfortable level.

Where can I read the privacy details?

See the Wake Up Verse Privacy Policy.
