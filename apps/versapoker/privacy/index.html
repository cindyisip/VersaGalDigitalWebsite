Privacy Policy

How the app stores poker hand histories, shares exports, and processes store purchases.

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Overview

Versa Poker Live Notes and Stats is a hand-recording and analysis tool from VersaGal Digital. The app does not run poker games, accept bets, move money, or connect to poker-room accounts. This policy covers the current development app, its browser mockup, and support communications.

Information stored in the app

You may enter poker sessions and hand details such as casino or location names, game stakes, dates and times, player names or labels, seat and position, stack sizes, hole cards, board cards, actions, bet amounts, notes, results, and session statistics. The app also stores settings, trial status, and the purchase-unlock status returned by the app store.

Local storage, exports, and deletion

Native iOS and Android versions store hand-history data in an on-device SQLite database. The browser mockup stores its data in that browser’s local storage. The current app has no account, cloud backup, server sync, or online hand-history service. Your records remain on the device unless you export or share them.

You can export hand-history information as a file and clear records in the app’s Settings. If you share an export, the destination you select receives the file under its own privacy practices. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes the app’s local copy; operating-system backups or exported copies may remain separately.

Network connections and providers

Live hand entry and saved history work offline. An internet connection is used for app-store purchase and restore requests. Apple App Store or Google Play processes those transactions and may receive transaction and device information under its own privacy policy. VersaGal Digital receives the store’s purchase or entitlement result, not your full payment-card number.

The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics in the current development build. The website and support email are operated separately; normal website or email providers may process connection details and the information you choose to send.

Data security

Hand histories are kept in the app’s device storage and are not transmitted to VersaGal Digital by the current app. Access to local data depends on the security of your device and its operating system. Protect your device with its available passcode and backup settings. Store transactions are handled through the platform’s app-store services.

Retention

Local records stay on your device until you clear them, remove the app or its data, or replace the device. Exported files and device backups are controlled by you and the services you choose, and may remain after the app copy is removed.

Children’s privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and the current version does not create an online account or send hand records to our servers. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through support, contact us so we can review and delete the message where appropriate.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if the app’s data practices change. For privacy questions or requests concerning support messages, email privacy@versagaldigital.com. For product help, visit Versa Poker Live Notes and Stats Support.
