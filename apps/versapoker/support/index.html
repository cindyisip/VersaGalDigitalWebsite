Frequently asked questions

Is Versa Poker available on the App Store?

Not yet. Versa Poker Live Notes and Stats is in development for iPhone. Public App Store availability and a download link will be posted on the product page when they are ready.

How does the Rapid Betting Numpad work?

The numpad is designed to capture the key shape of the hand quickly. Enter the largest bet faced on a street, choose tags that describe the action, and use the player and fold controls to record who remains. Add a street note for context the tags don’t capture.

It is a fast note-taking tool; review the saved hand details after play if you need to add or correct context.

Where are my sessions and hand notes stored?

They are stored locally on this device. The current app does not use an account or cloud sync. In Settings, export a CSV hand history or a full JSON data backup to keep a copy elsewhere.

What is included in the trial and full unlock?

The trial includes one session and up to 30 saved hands. The full-app unlock is a one-time US$9.99 purchase. If you already purchased it, use Restore Purchase in Settings.

Will a TestFlight unlock test charge me?

TestFlight in-app purchases use Apple’s sandbox testing environment and don’t charge a real payment method. The purchase sheet may still appear as part of the test.

What should I include when reporting a problem?

Include the app version/build, iPhone model, iOS version, session and hand number if available, and the steps that led to the issue. Crop or cover personal information in screenshots and avoid sending full hand-history backups unless they are needed to investigate.
