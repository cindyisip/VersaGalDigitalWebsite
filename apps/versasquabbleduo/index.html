How to play

Flip. Find. Squabble.

  1. Reveal the possibilities

    Take turns flipping randomly oriented tiles into the shared pool. Watch for words that meet the minimum length you selected.

  2. Build it, then tap it

    Tap I Found a Word and start choosing letters immediately. You get a brief 3…2…1… chance to cancel an accidental claim. Use Clear to restart your selection; once the assembled word is valid, tap the word itself to claim it.

  3. Steal the next point

    Build a different word using all the letters of an existing word, plus other available letters. Simply adding S is not a valid steal, and a word already used cannot be claimed again that round.
