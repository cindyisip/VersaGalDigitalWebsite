How to play

Flip. Find. Squabble.

  1. Reveal the possibilities

    Take turns flipping tiles into the shared pool. Both players watch for words of four or more letters.

  2. Beat the countdown

    Buzz when you spot a word, then select its letters and claim it before your timer runs out.

  3. Steal the next point

    Build a different word using all the letters of an existing word, plus other available letters. A word already used cannot be claimed again that round.
