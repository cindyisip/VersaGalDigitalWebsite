What VersaTally does

VersaTally helps you record shared expenses, see balances and suggested payments, and keep track of what has been settled. VersaTally does not hold, send or receive money and is not a bank, payment service or financial adviser. Payment buttons only open third-party apps such as Venmo, Cash App, PayPal or Zelle, whose own terms apply to any payment you make there. Balances depend on what you and the people you share with enter, so please check amounts before you pay or mark something settled.

Accounts

You need an account to use VersaTally; your expenses, receipts and attached files are stored with your account so they are available on your devices. You must give accurate information, keep access to your email account secure, and are responsible for activity under your account. You must be old enough to form a binding contract where you live. You can delete your account at any time; see Delete your VersaTally account and “Shared records stay intact” below.

Your content and sharing

You keep ownership of the information you enter. You give us permission to store, process and display it as needed to run VersaTally, including showing it to the people you share it with in Trips, Households and shared General expenses. Receipts and files attached to an expense can be seen by everyone on that expense. Expense notes are a permanent record for the group and cannot be edited or deleted after they are added. A scanned receipt that an expense was created from stays with that expense and cannot be removed; other attached files can be removed by the person who added them. Do not enter information you do not have the right to share, and do not use VersaTally to harass anyone, commit fraud, or break the law.

Shared records stay intact

Shared Trips, Households and General expenses shared with other people are a financial record for everyone in them. By taking part in one, you agree that the expenses, shares, payments, settlements, notes, receipts and attached files you are part of are kept for the other people, even if you later leave or delete your account.

When you delete your account, your place in those shared records is taken by a Proxy, a participant without an account. If you joined a group by claiming a Proxy, that Proxy is restored; otherwise a new Proxy is created for you. Every amount, share and settlement stays exactly as it was, so no one else’s balance changes. Your sign-in account and profile are deleted, but the name other members saved for you, or the name shown on those expenses, may still appear in their records. Shared expenses cannot be deleted just because one participant deletes their account.

Trips, Households and General expenses that no one else with a VersaTally account is part of (for example, ones with only you, or only you and people without an account) are deleted with your account, together with their notes, receipts and attached files.

Free use, Trip Pass and Unlimited

  • Free. Create up to 10 General expenses per calendar month. Trips can be created and joined for free.
  • Trip Pass. A one-time purchase (US$2.99 in the United States) that unlocks one Trip for all of its members. Any member can buy it, and it is not transferable to another Trip.
  • VersaTally Unlimited. An auto-renewing subscription (US$3.99 per month or US$29.99 per year in the United States) that removes the General expense limit and unlocks the Trips and Households the subscriber belongs to, for all of their members. Households require an active Unlimited subscription by at least one member to add or edit expenses.

Prices in other countries are shown in the App Store or Google Play before you buy and may include tax. We may change prices or features in the future; price changes for existing subscriptions follow Apple’s and Google’s rules, including any notice and consent they require.

Subscriptions, trials and billing

  • Unlimited renews automatically at the same price and length unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • Eligible new subscribers get a 7-day free trial. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the subscription starts and you are charged. Any unused part of a free trial ends when you purchase a subscription.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions). Deleting the app or your VersaTally account does not cancel a subscription.
  • Family Sharing is not available for Unlimited.

When a subscription ends

If Unlimited ends, Trips it already unlocked stay usable until they end or are settled. Households stay visible but become read-only until a member subscribes again. Settling up remains available. Your existing records are not deleted.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple or Google, and refund requests are handled by them under their policies. If a purchase is refunded, the feature it unlocked is removed, but the expense history stays readable.
