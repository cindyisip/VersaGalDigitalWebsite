Delete Your Account

How to delete your VersaTally account and what happens to your data.

Effective date: November 19, 2026

On this pageDelete your account in the appRequest deletion by emailWhat is deletedWhat is kept

Delete your account in the app

VersaTally is made by VersaGal Digital. The quickest way to delete your account is in the app:

  1. Open VersaTally and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open your Profile.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

Deletion takes effect right away. If you have an Unlimited subscription, cancel it in your Apple ID or Google Play settings, because deleting your account does not cancel billing.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app, email privacy@versagaldigital.com with the subject “VersaTally account deletion”. Send it from the email address on your VersaTally account so we can confirm the request. We will delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account, email address, display name and handle.
  • Your payment app handles (Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, Zelle).
  • Your VersaTally contacts and pending invitations.
  • Your private General expenses stored on our servers.

What is kept

  • Shared Trips and Households. Expenses, settlements and notes you added stay available to the other members, because they are part of the group’s financial record. They are no longer linked to your account.
  • Purchase records. Records of purchases may be kept as long as required for tax and accounting. Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep their own records under their policies.
  • Information on your device. Expenses and attachments stored only on your device, and any exports you made, stay there until you delete the app or its data.

See the VersaTally Privacy Policy for more detail.
