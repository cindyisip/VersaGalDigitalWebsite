Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in? Is there a password?

There is no password. Enter your email and VersaTally sends a one-time code, or use Sign in with Apple. Signing in and creating an account are separate: if you are new, choose to create an account and pick a display name. If a code doesn’t arrive, check your spam folder and wait a minute before requesting another.

What kinds of expenses can I organize?

Trips hold travel costs. Households hold shared home bills, organized by month, with recurring expenses carried forward. General holds everyday expenses that don’t belong to a Trip or Household.

How do I share a Trip or Household with friends?

Add friends who have VersaTally accounts in Groups → Contacts using their email address, then include them when you create or edit a Trip or Household. They accept the invitation in their Profile. People without an account can be added as Proxies; you can later invite a Proxy to claim their history when they sign up.

Why don’t I see changes my friend just made?

VersaTally refreshes shared data when the app opens or comes back to the foreground, and when you tap Refresh. Make sure you’ve accepted the invitation, and that the Trip or Household is unlocked. In Groups → Contacts, Check Sharing shows the status of each person you share with.

What’s free, and what do Trip Pass and Unlimited include?

Free accounts can create 10 General expenses a month. A Trip Pass (a one-time purchase) unlocks one Trip for everyone in it, and any member can buy it. VersaTally Unlimited (monthly or yearly, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers) removes the General limit and unlocks every Trip and Household you belong to, for all of their members. Households need at least one member with Unlimited to add or edit expenses. See the Terms of Use for full details.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → VersaTally. On Android, open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel billing. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

A purchase isn’t showing up. What should I do?

Make sure you’re signed in to the same VersaTally account, and the same Apple ID or Google account, that made the purchase. Then open Plan & Access in the app and restore purchases. If it still isn’t showing, email support with the date of the purchase. Don’t send card details.

Does receipt scanning save items automatically?

No. Scanning reads the receipt on your phone, even offline, and is a starting point. Review the merchant, total and line items, then import as a total or as itemized lines and assign each item to the people who had it. If the reading looks wrong, try another scan method.

Does VersaTally send or receive money?

No. VersaTally records expenses, balances and payment status. On Settle Up, payment buttons open Venmo, Cash App, PayPal or Zelle so you can pay there, then you mark the payment as paid or received in VersaTally. In-app purchases are separate from payments between friends.

Can I export my expenses?

Yes. Share on Settle Up offers a text summary or a spreadsheet-style CSV with every expense, each person’s share, totals and suggested payments. My Spending can also be shared as a summary or CSV.

Can I edit or delete an expense note?

No. Notes are a permanent record so the whole group can see what was agreed. Add a new note to correct or update one.

Where is my data stored?

When you’re signed in, your Trips, Households and expenses are stored on our servers so they sync across your devices and the people you share with. Receipt photos and other attachments stay on your device. Expenses you created before signing in stay on the device and aren’t uploaded. Read the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

Open your Profile in the app and choose Delete account, or follow the steps on the account deletion page. Shared expenses are never changed or removed: your place in each shared Trip or Household is taken by a Proxy, so everyone’s balances stay the same. The name shown for you on those records may remain.

What should I send with a bug report?

The VersaTally version from Settings, your device model, operating-system version, and the steps that reproduce the issue. Crop personal information from screenshots.
