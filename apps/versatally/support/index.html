Product overview

Frequently asked questions

Is VersaTally available to download?

VersaTally is still in development and is not yet available for public download. Availability will be announced on the product page when a release is ready.

What kinds of expenses can I organize?

VersaTally organizes travel costs in Trips, ongoing and one-time household costs by Household Space and month, and everyday expenses in General. Contexts keep related expenses and balances together.

Can I split an expense in different ways?

Yes. The app supports equal and customized participant shares, payer splits, and itemized receipt assignments. Review the expense details before saving or sharing them.

Does receipt scanning save items automatically?

No. Receipt scanning is a starting point. Review and correct the recognized merchant, total, currency, and line items before using the results in an expense.

Does VersaTally transfer money between people?

No. VersaTally records expenses, balances, and payment status. It does not transfer money or connect to bank accounts. App Store or Google Play purchases for app features are separate from payments between participants.

Where is my expense data stored?

The current development build keeps expense data on your device. Settings includes a portable JSON backup and on-device recovery options. Keep a backup somewhere safe if you need to move data between devices.

Will signing in upload my existing or sample expenses?

No. Account sign-in and cross-device syncing are still in development. Signing in does not automatically upload existing or sample expenses. Any future transfer will require a clear review and confirmation step.

What happens to a shared context if its owner deletes their account?

Account deletion and context handoff are being built for the public release. The intended behavior is to remove the sign-in account while preserving shared context history for its remaining members. Context editing will be available to a member who paid the context access entitlement or has VersaTally Plus. A member who is not an eligible context editor will not be able to edit expense details. The person receiving a payment may force-mark the related expense or balance as settled, and that recipient is the final settler. This handoff workflow is not available in the current development build.

Where can I read the VersaTally privacy policy?

The App Store-ready privacy policy is being prepared alongside account deletion and shared-data transfer. It will be published here and linked inside the app before public release. This support page is not a privacy policy.

What should I send with a bug report?

Send the app build, device model, operating-system version, and a short sequence of steps that reproduces the issue. Crop personal information from screenshots and avoid sharing full backups or private expense information.
