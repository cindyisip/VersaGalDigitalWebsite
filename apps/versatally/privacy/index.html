Privacy Policy

What VersaTally collects, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Overview

VersaTally is a shared-expense app made by VersaGal Digital (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. VersaTally records expenses, balances and payments between people. It never connects to bank accounts and never moves money.

We do not sell your information, show ads, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Information we collect

Account information. To share expenses you create an account with your email address and a display name and handle you choose. You can sign in with a one-time code sent to your email or with Sign in with Apple. With Sign in with Apple, we receive an identifier from Apple and the email address Apple provides, which may be a private relay address.

Shared expense information. When you are signed in, the Trips, Households and expenses you create or join are stored on our servers so they are available on your devices and to the people you share them with. This includes context names and dates, expense descriptions, dates, amounts, currencies and exchange rates, who paid and who shares each cost, itemized receipt lines and their assignments, settlements and payment status, invitations, and the names of Proxies (people without an account) you add.

Contacts you add in VersaTally. When you add another VersaTally account by email, we store that connection. VersaTally does not read your phone’s address book.

Expense notes. Notes you add to a shared expense are stored with your name and the time you added them.

Payment app handles. If you add Venmo, Cash App, PayPal or Zelle details to your profile, we store them so people you share expenses with can open those apps to pay you.

Purchases. If you buy a Trip Pass or subscribe to VersaTally Unlimited, the App Store or Google Play processes the payment. We and our subscription service, RevenueCat, receive the product, price, purchase and renewal dates and status, linked to your VersaTally account. We never receive your card or bank details.

Information that stays on your device

Receipt scanning. Receipts are read on your device using Apple Vision on iOS or Google ML Kit on Android, with an on-device fallback engine. Receipt photos are not sent to us as part of a scan. The first time the fallback engine is used, the app may download its language data over HTTPS; that download does not include your photo.

Attachments. Receipt photos and other attachments you add to an expense are stored on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

Expenses created before you sign in. Expenses you created on the device while signed out stay on the device. Signing in does not upload them.

Exports and backups. CSV exports, shared summaries and backups go only where you choose to send or save them, and are then subject to that destination’s privacy practices.

How we use information

We use the information above only to provide and support VersaTally: to sign you in, store and sync your expenses, show shared Trips and Households to their members, calculate balances and suggested payments, deliver invitations, provide the features you have unlocked, prevent abuse, and answer your support requests. We do not use it for advertising or sell it.

Who can see your information

People you share with. Members of a Trip or Household can see its expenses, balances, settlements and notes, and the display names and handles of the other members. Your payment app handles are shown to people you share expenses with so they can pay you. Pending invitees cannot see a context’s expenses until they accept.

Service providers. We use a small number of providers who process information on our behalf and only to run the app:

  • Supabase, for the database, sign-in and sending sign-in codes.
  • RevenueCat, to verify purchases and manage subscriptions.
  • Apple and Google, for Sign in with Apple, app distribution and in-app purchases, under their own privacy policies.

Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users or VersaGal Digital.

Retention and account deletion

We keep your account and shared information while your account is active. You can delete your account from your Profile in the app, or by following the steps on the Delete your VersaTally account page.

When you delete your account, we delete your sign-in account, profile, payment app handles, VersaTally contacts and your private General expenses. Shared Trips and Households are a financial record for the whole group, so expenses, settlements and notes you added to them stay available to the remaining members, no longer linked to your account. Purchase records may be kept as required for tax and accounting, and Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep their own records under their policies.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to shared data is enforced on our servers, so each account can read only the Trips, Households and expenses it belongs to. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so please protect your device with a passcode and keep your email account secure.

Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your profile in the app, remove payment app handles, leave or remove people from contexts you own (subject to the app’s rules for active expenses), and delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information. To make a request, email privacy@versagaldigital.com from the address on your VersaTally account.

Where information is processed

VersaGal Digital is based in the United States. Your information may be processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate.

Children’s privacy

VersaTally is intended for adults managing shared travel and household expenses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will post the new version here with a new effective date, and tell you in the app about material changes. For privacy questions, email privacy@versagaldigital.com. For help with the app, visit VersaTally Support.
