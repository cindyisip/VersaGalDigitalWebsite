Privacy Policy

How the current development build handles expense records, receipt scans, and exports.

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Overview

VersaTally is a shared-expense app in development. This policy describes the current development build. It records amounts and settlement status; it does not connect to bank accounts or move money between people.

Information in the current build

The app stores expense information on your device, including expense spaces and names, dates, amounts, currencies and exchange rates, payer and participant names, item details, split assignments, settlement status and history, notes, settings, and receipt images or other attachments you add. Sample data may also be present in a development build.

Receipt scanning

When you scan a receipt, the image is processed on your device. Native versions use Apple Vision on iOS and Google ML Kit on Android. The app does not send the image to VersaGal Digital or an OCR server as part of a scan. The first OCR scan may download the OCR engine or language data over HTTPS; the delivery service may receive standard request information such as your IP address, but the receipt image is not part of that download request. You review and edit recognized details before saving them.

Storage, exports, and sharing

The current build stores expense information locally in the app. Sign-in, cloud synchronization, account sharing, and billing are not connected to the current release; local records are not automatically uploaded when you use simulated sign-in screens. Device backups may include app data according to your operating-system settings.

You can create a JSON backup or CSV export. A JSON backup may include attachments. When you share or save an export, the file goes to the destination you choose and is then subject to that destination’s privacy practices. Removing the app or its data removes the local copy, but does not necessarily remove exported files or separate device backups.

Purchases and future services

The current development build does not process real purchases. Any unlock or billing flow shown in a mockup or testing build is simulated. Account sign-in, cloud transfer, and purchase services are still in development. If those features are added, we will update this policy to describe the information sent to providers and how you can manage or delete it before public release.

Data security

Expense records and receipt images are stored in the app’s device storage. The app does not maintain a server copy in the current development build. Protect your device with its available passcode and backup settings. OCR component downloads use HTTPS; the image itself is not sent with that request.

Retention

Local records remain on your device until you clear them, remove the app or its data, or replace the device. Exported files and device backups may persist separately under your control or the terms of the service you use.

Children’s privacy

VersaTally is designed for people organizing shared household and travel expenses and is not directed to children under 13. The current build has no online account and does not transmit local expense records to us. If you believe a child has sent personal information through support, contact us so we can review it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as development and data practices change. For privacy questions, email privacy@versagaldigital.com. For help, visit VersaTally Support.
