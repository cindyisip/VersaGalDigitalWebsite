Good to know

Built for a clearer picture.

Do I need a DUPR account?

Yes, live player lookups require your DUPR account and any verification requested during sign-in. Availability depends on DUPR’s service and access requirements.

Can I use a roster from CourtReserve?

You can bring names you have copied or screenshots you choose. VersaPickle Tools does not connect directly to your CourtReserve account.

Will a name always identify the correct player?

No. People can share names, and a profile’s location may differ from where someone is playing. Review profile details and ambiguous matches. Widen the search area if a visitor isn’t appearing.

Does a lookup automatically save the roster?

No. New lookups are temporary until you choose Save roster. You can check results and discard the list. Save anything you want to keep before closing the app.

Does it track matches or predict who will win?

The first tool focuses on roster lookup and player ratings. Match tracking and outcome prediction are not part of this initial feature.

VersaPickle Tools is an independent VersaGal Digital project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by DUPR or CourtReserve. Ratings come from the connected service and should be reviewed in context.
