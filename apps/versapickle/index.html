Lookup Rosters

Paste names or import roster screenshots. Review profile matches, narrow searches by location, and compare available singles and doubles ratings. Save a roster on your phone or export it as CSV.

Explore available history with a graph you can scroll and zoom. Tap a match or event to see the details, including your partner, opponents, rating movement, and ending rating.

Understand your history coverage

One connected account

Use your own DUPR account and reconnect with the same identity. No additional VersaPickle sign-up.

History on your phone

Downloaded matches stay on your device. Searching saved matches and exploring the graph do not request data from DUPR.

Clear about the limits

Your results depend on the records available to your DUPR account and the history you have downloaded.

Your DUPR access determines what’s available.

VersaPickle can only display and analyze data DUPR makes available to your connected account. If your DUPR access is limited, the corresponding features and statistics in VersaPickle will also be limited.

Read about data availability and responsible use

VersaPickle Ratings is an independent VersaGal Digital app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by DUPR or CourtReserve. DUPR supplies the ratings; VersaPickle helps you explore the available records.
