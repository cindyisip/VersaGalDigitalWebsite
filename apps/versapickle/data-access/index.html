DUPR & your data

What’s available, what’s stored, and how to read your results.

On this pageAccount accessDemo and app tiersHistory coverageRatings & statisticsPersonal useIndependent app

Your account determines access

VersaPickle can only display and analyze data that DUPR makes available to your connected account. If DUPR limits your access to ratings or match history, the corresponding features and statistics in VersaPickle will also be limited.

VersaPickle does not upgrade your DUPR account or bypass its access restrictions. Use your own account and reconnect with that same identity. The current app does not support switching to another player’s account.

Demo data and app tiers

Demo mode uses fictional players, rosters, and matches. It does not connect to DUPR, validate a real player’s rating, or change the data associated with your account. The Demo mode banner and exported sample labels identify these results.

The planned free tier and Ratings Plus also determine which app features and downloaded records you can use. Free includes three other roster players plus your own profile and your latest five downloaded matches across singles and doubles combined. Ratings Plus adds larger rosters, older available history, and real Match Lookup.

Ratings Plus unlocks VersaPickle features, not additional DUPR permissions. Neither a purchase nor a demo can provide records DUPR does not return. See the planned feature comparison; development builds may differ while release details are finalized.

Available does not mean downloaded

Progress graphs and Match Lookup use history already downloaded to your iPhone. With Ratings Plus, continue loading older history to broaden the date range. A two-year graph setting does not fetch two years of matches automatically.

Reaching the end of returned history means DUPR has returned no more records for that request. It is not a guarantee that every match you have ever played is included. Missing records, unsupported formats, and account restrictions can affect coverage.

Saved history can become out of date. Recent refreshes check newer records; older edits or removals may require a full history recheck. Red dashed graph connectors compress longer gaps between downloaded matches and do not prove why a gap exists.

Ratings and statistics need context

DUPR supplies the ratings. VersaPickle does not calculate an official DUPR rating or fill in missing historical ratings with your current rating. NR indicates unavailable historical information.

Matchup statistics describe the supported, downloaded records. A match can include multiple games, and a small sample does not establish who is your best partner or toughest opponent. Check the count and date coverage before comparing results.

For an incorrect DUPR score, rating, or account record, contact DUPR Support. For how something is displayed in VersaPickle, contact VersaGal Digital.

Use the connection responsibly

VersaPickle is intended for personal use. Do not abuse the DUPR connection or repeatedly retry requests during a restriction. Excessive requests in a short period may be flagged or restricted by DUPR. Because your device connects directly to DUPR, its restrictions and enforcement apply to your account.

Consult DUPR’s terms and privacy policy. This disclosure does not override those requirements.

An independent app

VersaPickle Ratings is developed by VersaGal Digital and is not affiliated with or endorsed by DUPR or CourtReserve. It imports names or screenshots you select; it does not connect directly to CourtReserve.

The app is currently in development and is not yet available on the App Store. Release details will be posted on the product page.

For how the app handles account information, storage, and deletion, read the VersaPickle privacy policy.
