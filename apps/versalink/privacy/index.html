Overview

VersaLink is developed and published by VersaGal Digital. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the VersaLink mobile application.

The short version: VersaLink is designed as a local-first app. Your saved links and organization data remain on your device unless you intentionally export them.

Data stored in VersaLink

VersaLink does not require an account. The app stores the links you add, along with related titles, notes, categories, tags, and preferences, locally on your device.

VersaGal Digital does not operate a server that receives or stores your VersaLink library. The app is not designed to sell personal information or use your saved content for advertising.

In-app purchases

If you purchase the VersaLink one-time upgrade, Apple processes the transaction through the App Store. VersaGal Digital does not receive your full payment-card details. Apple may provide transaction and entitlement information needed to confirm your purchase. Apple handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Exported files

If you choose to export your VersaLink data, the exported file is created at your request and handled through the destination you select on your device. You are responsible for protecting exported files and deciding where they are stored or shared.

Support communications

If you contact us for support, we receive the information you voluntarily include in your message, such as your email address, device details, screenshots, or description of the problem. We use it only to respond and troubleshoot.

Children’s privacy

VersaLink is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when VersaLink’s features or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about privacy may be sent to privacy@versagaldigital.com.
