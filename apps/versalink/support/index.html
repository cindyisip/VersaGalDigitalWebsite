VersaLink Support

Help with saving, organizing, exporting, restoring, and unlocking your link library.

Contact support

Email support@versagaldigital.com and include a brief description of what happened.

Helpful details include your iPhone model, iOS version, VersaLink version, and the steps that led to the issue. Please do not send private links or exported files unless they are necessary to investigate the problem.

Where is my data stored?

VersaLink stores your library locally on your device. It does not require an account or automatically synchronize your links to a VersaGal Digital server.

How do backups work?

VersaLink maintains local recovery copies and keeps the seven most recent daily copies. For protection outside the device, use Export and save the resulting file somewhere you control.

Purchases and restoration

The one-time VersaLink upgrade is handled by Apple. If you previously purchased the upgrade with the same Apple Account, use the app’s Restore Purchase option.

Privacy

Read the VersaLink Privacy Policy for details about local storage, exports, purchases, and support communications.
