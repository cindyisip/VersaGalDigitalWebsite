Save it. Sort it. Find it.

Built around your collection

Less searching for the link.
More enjoying the find.

01

Keep it together

Paste a URL into the app, add a title or note, and choose a folder. Links without a folder stay in Unsorted.

02

Make it your own

Use colored folders and tags, reorder your folders, and keep favorites close. Choose Soft Lavender or another background across the app.

03

Find it again

Search titles, URLs, notes, tags, and folders. Archive links for later, or move, copy, share, and delete several at once.

04

Recognize the link

See titles and thumbnails when a website provides them, including supported YouTube videos. Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok previews depend on what is publicly available.

05

Keep control

No app account, ads, or analytics. Your collection lives on your device. Optional previews contact the linked services; turn automatic fetching off in Settings.

06

Take a backup

Export a JSON backup or a CSV for spreadsheets. Local recovery copies offer another way back, with import and recovery restore included in the lifetime unlock.
