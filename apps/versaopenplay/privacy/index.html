Privacy Policy

How the testing service handles player profiles, pickleball records, groups, and tournaments.

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Overview

Versa Open Play is a pickleball game-history, player-statistics, group, and tournament service currently in testing. It requires an internet connection and an account for sign-in and saving. This policy covers the testing service and related support.

Account and profile information

When you create an account, the authentication service processes your email address and sign-in credentials. Your profile may include first and last name, display name, unique handle, city, state or region, country, year of birth, gender for division eligibility, self-reported rating, a DUPR rating you provide, playing-club choices, and privacy and statistics preferences. Year of birth is used for age-related division and statistics features; the app does not need your full date of birth.

Games, groups, tournaments, and messages

The service stores pickleball game records, including scores, date and time, court or location, game type, participating players, verification and correction history, and related statistics. It may also store guest or proxy player names, club and group memberships, group roles, tournament registrations, schedules, divisions, results, bracket progress, placements, and organizer actions. If you submit a message through Feedback & Support, we store the subject, category, message, and support status so the support administrator can respond.

Location information

You can choose a profile city and clubs. The app uses those selected places for discovery and approximate-distance features. It does not require precise GPS location or run background location tracking. Game locations and courts are recorded when entered or selected by users.

How we use information

We use account and profile data to authenticate users, display profiles according to your visibility settings, connect players, create and verify game records, calculate statistics and leaderboards, manage groups and tournaments, maintain account security, and answer support requests. We do not use player or game records for targeted advertising.

Who can see information

Profile visibility can be set to public, group members only, or game participants only. Game details are visible to the players who need them to record or verify a result. Information in groups and tournaments is shown to the relevant members, players, organizers, and co-admins. Public profile, leaderboard, or event information may be visible to anyone when the related feature or visibility setting is public. Choose display names and privacy settings with care.

Service providers and sharing

Supabase provides authentication, database, and backend services; Cloudflare delivers the web app and related network services. These providers process information on our behalf to operate the testing service. Email providers deliver sign-in and account messages. Other users receive the profile, game, group, and tournament information described above based on your choices and their role. We do not sell personal information or include third-party advertising in the current testing service.

Data security

Connections to the service use HTTPS. Database access controls restrict information to authorized account and group scopes, and access is limited to what is needed to operate the service and provide support. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so use a strong, unique password and do not reuse another service’s password.

Retention and deletion

You can request account deletion in the app where the option is available, or email us at privacy@versagaldigital.com. If you own groups, you may need to transfer ownership or delete those groups first. Deletion closes account access and removes or redacts identifying profile information. To preserve the accuracy of shared match and community history, we may retain game scores, rating snapshots, membership history, tournament results, and a reserved handle in a de-identified or former-player form. Support messages and security records may be kept as needed to handle the request, resolve disputes, protect the service, or meet legal requirements. Backup copies may persist for the provider’s normal backup cycle.

Children’s privacy

The current testing service is intended for people age 13 or older and is not directed to children under 13. The profile form asks for year of birth, but the testing service does not provide a parental-consent flow. People under 13 should not create an account. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account or submitted personal information, contact us so we can review the account and take appropriate action.

Changes and contact

We may revise this policy as the testing service changes. For privacy requests, email privacy@versagaldigital.com. For general questions or help, email support@versagaldigital.com.
