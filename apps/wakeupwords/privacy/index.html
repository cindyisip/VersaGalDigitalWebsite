Wake Up Words

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Wake Up Words handles information in the app.

Effective date: November 17, 2026

In brief: Wake Up Words doesn’t collect any data. Your messages, folders, alarms, and settings stay on your device. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, and the app makes no internet connections. The free trial and the optional one-time purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store.

Information stored on your device

Wake Up Words stores what it needs to work: the messages you write or edit, your folders, favorites, alarm times and repeat settings, playlist, Nightstand and Message Frame choices, voice, appearance, and text-size preferences, and the alarm sounds it prepares. This information is kept in the app’s storage on your device. It is not sent to VersaGal Digital or anyone else.

No internet connection

The app doesn’t connect to the internet. It has no server, no account, and no online lookups. Its starter messages and background music are included in the app.

Speech and background music

Your messages are read aloud by the voices installed on your iPhone, on your device. To let you choose a voice, the app reads the list of voices installed on your device; that list stays on your device. Background music is included with the app and played locally.

Free trial and in-app purchase

Wake Up Words offers a free 7-day trial and an optional one-time purchase that unlocks the full app. Both are handled by Apple through the App Store. Starting the trial is recorded as a free in-app purchase so the App Store can remember when it began, including after you reinstall the app. The app reads this trial and purchase status from the App Store on your device to decide which features are available. We don’t receive your payment details, and checking your purchase doesn’t send your messages, alarms, or settings to us. Apple handles purchase information under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Alarms and permissions

The app uses Apple’s alarm and audio features. iOS may ask your permission to schedule alarms and keeps alarm schedules in its own system services, under Apple’s privacy terms and your settings. Nightstand and Message Frame keep the screen on while they’re showing and don’t collect any information.

Diagnostics, analytics, and advertising

The app has no advertising, tracking, or analytics. If a future version adds any connected service, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Retention and deletion

Your information stays on your device until you delete it in the app, or delete the app. Device backups (such as iCloud Backup) may include app data according to your backup settings.

Children’s privacy

Wake Up Words is a general audience app and doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children. Parents or guardians can contact us with privacy questions.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above will change when an update is posted.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@versagaldigital.com. For app support, contact support@versagaldigital.com.
