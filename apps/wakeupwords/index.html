Made for anyone

A friend in your alarm clock.

Wake Up Words turns the first sound of your day into the words you need to hear, and keeps cheering you on until bedtime.

1

Your own words, read aloud

Write the messages you want to hear, or start with eight folders of fresh, uplifting ones. Your iPhone reads them in a warm, natural voice.

2

Folders you control

Morning Motivation, Affirmations, Calm & Breathe, Focus & Work, Health & Habits, Gratitude, Wind Down and My Reminders. Add, rename, recolor, hide or delete them.

3

A different message each morning

Each alarm reads one message, a random message from a folder, or a random favorite, so mornings don’t get stale.

4

Read along as it’s spoken

Tap Open when the alarm rings: your message appears in large type, each word lighting up as it’s read, with Snooze and Stop.

5

Reminders through the day

Repeat an alarm every 15 minutes up to every 12 hours, until the time you choose. Drink water, stretch, breathe, refocus, with a new message each time.

6

Message Frame

Like a talking picture frame: it shows one message and reads each new one aloud with soft music, every few minutes.

7

Playlist and Nightstand

Play a folder or your favorites with a breathing pause between messages, or keep a silent bedside clock with one message at a time.

8

Voices and music you like

Choose any voice on your iPhone (with a Try button for each) and Warm Pad, Rain & Wind, or words only.

9

Bright, private, yours

Light and dark modes, adjustable text, no account, no ads, no tracking. Everything you write stays on your phone.

Nightstand & Message Frame

Your words, all day and all night.

Two ways to keep a good word in sight between alarms. Start either one from the Playlist tab.

Turn your phone sideways for a classic bedside or desk clock.

A look inside

Bright mornings, calm evenings.

A cheerful sunrise look in light and dark, with your messages in speech bubbles.
