Support

Help with alarms, folders and messages, voices, the playlist, Message Frame, and purchases.

Contact support
Email support@versagaldigital.com. If you can, include your device model, iOS version, app version (in Settings), and the steps you took. Please don’t include private message text unless it’s needed to explain the issue.

Common questions

How do the free trial and the unlock work?

Wake Up Words is free to download. You can try everything free for 7 days; nothing is charged automatically and there is no subscription. After the trial, the free version keeps one alarm that reads one message, the starter folders, and up to 3 messages of your own. A one-time purchase of US$4.99 unlocks unlimited alarms and messages, your own folders, random messages, and Repeat during the day. Regional prices and taxes may vary; Apple shows the final price before you confirm.

I already bought it. How do I unlock it again?

Open Settings → Full version and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought it with. A new iPhone or a reinstall needs this once. Alarms that the free version switched off turn back on after you unlock.

Why can’t I schedule alarms on my iPhone?

Alarms use Apple’s alarm system, which needs iOS 26 or later. On earlier versions, your alarms are saved and start ringing after you update; folders, the playlist, Nightstand, and Message Frame work meanwhile. To update, open Settings → General → Software Update. Not every iPhone can install iOS 26; Apple’s update guide explains how to check.

How do I add my own message?

Open Folders, pick a folder (My Reminders is empty and ready for you), type in Add a message, and tap Add message. Tap any message to edit it, move it to another folder, hear it, or delete it. Shorter messages work best for alarms: an iPhone alarm sound has to be under 30 seconds, so keep each message to about 40 words.

Can I hide or delete a folder?

Yes. Open the folder and tap Hide or Delete. A hidden folder keeps its messages but stays out of the playlist, Nightstand, favorites, and the alarm’s folder list; find it under Hidden folders to show it again. Deleting a folder also deletes its messages; alarms that used it switch to Random favorites.

How does an alarm choose its message?

In the alarm editor, under What it reads, choose One message, Random from a folder, or Random favorites (tap the star on any message to make it a favorite). A random alarm prepares its next message ahead of time and picks a new one when you open the app.

Can an alarm ring several times a day?

Yes. Turn on Also repeat during the day, right under the days, then choose minutes (every 15 to 55) or hours (every 1 to 12) and an end time. The editor lists the times it will ring, up to 48 a day. With a random alarm, turn on A different message each time to hear up to 8 different messages through the day.

What is Message Frame?

On the Playlist tab, tap Start Message Frame. It shows the time and one message, like a picture frame, and every 1 to 60 minutes it changes to a new message and reads it aloud with the background music. Tap the screen for Next message, Say it again, timing, music, brightness, and Exit. The screen stays on, so keep your phone charging.

What is Nightstand?

Also on the Playlist tab: a silent bedside clock that shows a new message every 1 to 60 minutes in warm, dim colors. A scheduled alarm still rings over it.

The voice sounds robotic. How do I get a better one?

Download a free natural voice: open iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Read & Speak (Spoken Content on iOS 17–18) → Voices → English, and download a Premium or Enhanced voice such as Ava, Zoe, Tom, or Evan. Back in Wake Up Words, open Settings → Reading voice and tap Try next to it. Each alarm can also have its own voice.

Why can’t I hear the spoken message?

  • Check the volume and the audio output, such as Bluetooth headphones or a speaker.
  • Try a different voice under Settings → Reading voice, and make sure it has finished downloading.
  • If it’s still silent, email support with your iPhone model and iOS version.

Why is the alarm quiet or not ringing?

Make sure the alarm is switched on and that Wake Up Words is allowed to use alarms (iPhone Settings → Wake Up Words). Check the volume. If it continues, send support the alarm time, its settings, and your device details.

Can I change the look and text size?

Yes. In Settings, choose Appearance (Automatic follows your iPhone’s Light or Dark setting) and drag Text size.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Wake Up Words never needs an internet connection. Your messages, the voices, and the music are all on your phone.
