iPhone Tester Guide

Thank you for helping test our alarm apps! This guide walks you through installing the beta with Apple’s TestFlight app. It takes about 3 minutes.

Using an Android phone? See the Android Tester Guide.

Before you start

  • Use the iPhone you want to test on, and open this page on that iPhone (Safari is best). The beta links only work on the device itself.
  • Alarms in both apps need iOS 26 or later (Settings → General → Software Update). On older iOS versions you can still try everything else.
  • You don’t need a code, an invitation email, or to send us your email address. The links below are all you need.

Install the beta

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store TestFlight is Apple’s free app for trying apps before they are released. Get TestFlight on the App Store, tap Get, and wait for it to finish installing.
  2. Don’t tap “Redeem” If you open TestFlight now, it may show an empty screen or a Redeem button asking for a code. Ignore it, you don’t need a code. Just close TestFlight (or switch back to Safari) and come back to this page.
  3. Tap the beta link for the app you want to test Tap one of the buttons below. You can test one or both apps.
    Wake Up Verse
    Open the Wake Up Verse beta
    Wake Up Words
    Open the Wake Up Words beta
    The link should open TestFlight. If it shows a web page instead, tap View in TestFlight or Start Testing on that page.
  4. Tap “Accept”, then “Install” TestFlight shows the app with a short description. Tap Accept, then Install. If you tested before, the button says Update or Open.
  5. Open the app The app appears on your Home Screen with a small orange dot next to its name; that dot means it’s a beta. On first launch, allow notifications and alarms when the app asks, so your alarms can ring.

To test the other app later, come back to this page and repeat steps 3 to 5 with its link. TestFlight only needs to be installed once.

What to try

  • Set an alarm a few minutes ahead, lock your iPhone, and check that it rings with the message or verse read aloud and the background music.
  • Try Snooze and Stop, an alarm on several days, and Repeat during the day.
  • Try the 7-day free trial and the unlock screen in Settings (see “Updates and purchases” below).
  • Use it like you normally would for a few days. Anything confusing, slow, or broken is worth telling us.

Send feedback

  • With a screenshot: take a screenshot in the app (side button + volume up), tap the thumbnail that appears, then tap Done (or the share button) and choose Share Beta Feedback. Add a short note about what happened.
  • From TestFlight: open TestFlight, tap the app, then Send Beta Feedback.
  • If the app crashes, TestFlight may ask whether to send a crash report. Please tap Share and say what you were doing.
  • By email: support@versagaldigital.com. Tell us your iPhone model and iOS version.

Updates and purchases

  • Updates: when we post a new beta, TestFlight sends a notification. Open TestFlight and tap Update, or turn on Automatic Updates on the app’s page in TestFlight.
  • Purchases are free in the beta. In TestFlight, the free trial and the US$4.99 unlock are test purchases: Apple shows a purchase sheet, but you are not charged.
  • Test purchases don’t carry over to the App Store version. When the app is released, install it from the App Store; the beta and its purchase stay in TestFlight.
  • Each beta build works for 90 days; we’ll post newer builds before then.

If something goes wrong

  • TestFlight asks for a code / “Redeem”: you don’t need one. Close TestFlight, come back to this page in Safari, and tap the app’s link again (step 3).
  • The link opens a web page with “Step 1: Get TestFlight”: TestFlight isn’t installed yet, or it hadn’t finished installing. Install it (step 1), then tap the link again.
  • “This beta isn’t accepting any new testers” or “This beta is full”: please email us and we’ll make room.
  • You opened the link on a computer: send it to yourself (Messages or email) and open it on your iPhone.
  • The alarm didn’t ring: check you’re on iOS 26 or later, that notifications and alarms are allowed for the app in Settings, and that the alarm is switched on. Then email us the alarm time and what happened.

Stop testing

You can stop at any time: open TestFlight, tap the app, scroll down, and tap Stop Testing. Then delete the app from your Home Screen if you like.

Questions? Email support@versagaldigital.com. Thank you for testing!
