Android Tester Guide

Thank you for helping test our alarm apps! Testing on Android happens through Google Play and takes three short steps: join our tester group, become a tester, and install. About 5 minutes.

Using an iPhone? See the iPhone Tester Guide.

The Android test is open. Google approved our test on November 19, 2026. Follow the steps below to join.

Before you start

  • You need an Android phone with the Google Play Store (Android 7 or later).
  • Open this page on that phone if you can.
  • Use one Google account for every step. It must be the account your Play Store uses. To check: open the Play Store app and tap your profile picture at the top right. The email shown there is the one to use.
The most common problem: joining the group with one Google account and opening the test link with another. If your phone or browser has more than one Google account, make sure the same account is selected each time.

Join the test

  1. Find your Play Store account As above: Play Store → profile picture. Remember that email address.
  2. Join the “Wake Up testers” Google Group Open groups.google.com/g/versagal-wakeup-testers, sign in with that same Google account if asked, and tap Join group. One group covers both apps. Only we can post in the group, and only for test news (such as “the test links are open”). Other testers can’t see your email address.
  3. Become a tester for each app you want to test Tap a button below. Google Play opens a page about the test; tap Become a tester. You should then see “You are now a tester”. If the page says the app isn’t available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.

  4. Install the app On the “You are now a tester” page, tap download it on Google Play (or use the Install from Google Play link under the app above), then tap Install. Test apps don’t show up when you search the Play Store, so use the link.
  5. Open the app and allow alarms On first launch, allow notifications. If the app shows an Alarm permissions card, tap it and allow Alarms & reminders and full-screen alerts, so the alarm can ring and show over the lock screen.

Why 14 days matters

Before a new app can go on Google Play for everyone, Google requires at least 12 testers to stay in the test for 14 days in a row. That’s why each tester counts so much. Please:

  • Keep the app installed and stay a tester for at least 14 days.
  • Open it every day or two, and let a real alarm ring now and then.
  • Tell us if you need to leave early, so we can find someone else.

What to try

  • Set an alarm a few minutes ahead, lock your phone, and check that it rings with the message or verse read aloud and the background music.
  • Try Snooze and Stop, an alarm on several days, and Repeat during the day.
  • Restart your phone and check that your alarms still ring.
  • Use it like you normally would. Anything confusing, slow, or broken is worth telling us.

Send feedback

  • Email: support@versagaldigital.com. Tell us your phone model and Android version, and add a screenshot if you can.
  • On Google Play: on the app’s Play Store page, testers can send private feedback that only we can see.

Updates and purchases

  • Updates arrive through the Play Store like any other app.
  • Please use the free 7-day trial for testing. On Android, the US$4.99 unlock in the test is a real purchase charged by Google Play. You don’t need to buy it to help us. If you do buy it, it stays yours when the app is released.

If something goes wrong

  • “App not found”, “Item not available”, or no “Become a tester” button: usually a Google account mix-up. Check that you joined the group with the same account your Play Store uses (step 1), wait a few minutes, and try again.
  • Several Google accounts in Chrome: open the test link in a private (Incognito) tab and sign in with your Play Store account, or switch accounts from the profile picture at the top of the page.
  • The Play Store page has no Install button: close the Play Store app completely, reopen the install link, and wait a minute. Google can take a little while to recognize a new tester.
  • The alarm didn’t ring or was silent: check that notifications and “Alarms & reminders” are allowed for the app, that battery saver isn’t restricting it, and that the alarm volume is up. Then email us the alarm time and what happened.

Stop testing

Open the app’s “Become a tester” link again and tap Leave the program, then uninstall the app if you like. If you can, please stay for the full 14 days. Thank you!

Questions? Email support@versagaldigital.com. Thank you for testing!
