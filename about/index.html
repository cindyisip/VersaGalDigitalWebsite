About

A home for ideas in more than one form.

VersaGal Digital is an independent creative brand exploring how technology and storytelling can become useful, meaningful digital experiences.

Why VersaGal Digital?

Some ideas solve a practical problem. Others become a song. VersaGal Digital was created to give both kinds of ideas—and the possibilities between them—a shared home.

What we create

Our work currently spans thoughtful mobile apps and original music, with room to grow into other digital products in the future.
